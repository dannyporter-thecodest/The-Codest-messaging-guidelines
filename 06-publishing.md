# 06. Publishing

Cody drafts. People publish. There is no automatic publication by any AI agent.

## Publishing path (every piece, no exceptions)

1. **Author** (or Cody) lists the claims used and their sources.
2. **Delivery** confirms project facts and removes anything that can't be disclosed.
3. **Marketing Specialist** edits into plain language, checks visibility levels and consent, and runs the confidentiality check.
4. **The right role approves usage.** For 1:1 sales use, that's the Business Growth & Partnerships Lead.
5. The piece gets a **version and a decision record**.
6. **Marketing Specialist publishes.** Publication is only possible with no BLOCKED claims and no missing fields.

## Expert interview playbook

Interview (30 to 45 min) → transcript → 3 to 5 topics → Delivery fact check → Marketing edit → confidentiality check → publish.

Cody can help with: transcription clean-up, picking topics, first drafts, repurposing one interview into several formats, checking a draft against these guidelines.

## Confidentiality: who is responsible

| Activity | Responsible | Confirms / approves |
|---|---|---|
| content and publication | Marketing Specialist | Delivery confirms facts |
| public use of client material | Marketing Specialist | formal client consent |
| anonymised use of client material | Marketing Specialist | formal client consent |
| 1:1 use in sales | Business Growth & Partnerships Lead | contract check and scope of consent |

**MUFG and Allegro:**
- public: never, without client consent
- anonymised public: a draft may be prepared, never published without separate consent
- 1:1 sales: only after a contract check and internal approval. A client saying "I think we can agree" is intent, not consent
- internal: full facts allowed, clearly labelled confidential

## AI use and the EU AI Act

AI can help with transcription, topic selection and editing. A human stays responsible for facts, confidentiality and publication. No client data goes into an AI tool without an approved basis and a security setup.

Four practical rules (Article 50 applies from 2 Aug 2026):

1. Chatbots or automated replies say at first contact that the user is talking to an AI system, unless that's obvious.
2. Deepfakes (realistic image, audio or video that could pass as real) are clearly labelled.
3. Text on matters of public interest goes through human review with named editorial responsibility. Otherwise AI use must be disclosed. The publishing path above meets this.
4. Every piece keeps a source log and a confidentiality check.

Using Claude Code, Codex or GitHub Copilot does not mean all code or marketing content must be labelled. These are operational guidelines, not legal advice.

## Pre-publish checklist

Cody runs this on every draft and reports the result.

- [ ] Audience group (3, 2, 1) and buying role named? Funnel stage?
- [ ] Leads with the client's problem, not our services, technology or AI?
- [ ] Supports the one promise without creating a second one?
- [ ] Claim IDs listed? Nothing BLOCKED? Nothing past its review date?
- [ ] Conditional claims only used if their condition is cleared and recorded?
- [ ] No MUFG or Allegro, named, anonymised or implied?
- [ ] No US market targeting? No GFT as a client?
- [ ] No invented or combined numbers?
- [ ] Limits and conditions sit next to promises?
- [ ] Nothing from the avoid list (see 04-voice.md)? No em dashes?
- [ ] AI described as specific practice with limits, not as the headline?
- [ ] One clear, small next step as the CTA?
- [ ] Translation keeps the same claim scope as the English source?
- [ ] Delivery has verified project facts?
- [ ] Source log kept; human review done?

## How content is measured

By movement to a conversation, account quality and opportunity value. Not by page views or number of posts. Every piece is tied to a group, a role and a funnel stage.

Funnel stages: **signal** (fits the ICP and there's a reason to reach out) → **qualified conversation** (problem, owner, business weight, next step) → **opportunity** (approximate value, timing, decision process, risks) → **offer** (scope, team model, price and decision date agreed) → **won / lost** (reason recorded and fed back into content and qualification).
